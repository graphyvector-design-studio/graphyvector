Common questions.

Everything you need to know before reaching out.

How fast do you respond? +
I personally review every message and aim to respond within 2–4 hours during the day. For urgent projects, WhatsApp or a direct call is the fastest route.
Do you work with clients outside India? +
Yes — the majority of my clients are international. I've worked with brands across the US, UK, UAE, Australia and Europe. All communication is in English and payments are handled internationally via Fiverr, Upwork or direct transfer.
What information should I prepare before contacting? +
A brief description of what you need, any references or inspiration you love, your approximate budget and your deadline. The more detail you share upfront, the faster and more accurately I can respond with a proposal.
What's your typical project timeline? +
It depends on scope — a logo or email signature can be done in 2–3 days, a brand identity system takes 6–8 weeks, and a full motion design project typically runs 2–4 weeks. I always share a clear timeline before starting.
Do you offer revisions? +
Yes — every project includes at least 1–2 rounds of revisions depending on the scope. I'd rather iterate until it's right than deliver something you're not 100% happy with.
Can I see more work before contacting? +
Absolutely — check out the Works section on this site, my Behance profile, LottieFiles page and Fiverr portfolio. If you want to see something specific (a certain style or niche), just mention it and I'll share relevant examples directly.
