Our standards

What separates good print
from great print.

01

Hierarchy that guides the eye

Every layout has a clear reading order — headline, subhead, body, CTA. The reader's eye is guided, never lost.

02

Correct bleed & safe zones

All files include 3mm bleed and safe zones so nothing important gets cut during production. Print-ready from day one.

03

CMYK color accuracy

We work in CMYK from the start — not converted at the end. Colors print the way they look on screen.

04

Typography that works at scale

Font choices that stay legible at 8pt body copy and still feel premium at 120pt headlines. Both matter in print.

Cover Story

Why a Cover is a Promise

A well-designed cover doesn't describe the book — it makes the reader feel something before a single page is turned.

ILLUSTRATION PLACEHOLDER · COVER DESIGN SAMPLE

Fig. 1 — Concept sketch to final cover, three-stage editorial process.

The cover is the first sensory contract between author and reader. Before a blurb is read, before a review is trusted, the image does its work in less than two seconds — genre, mood, quality, price point, all decoded subconsciously.
"The best covers don't illustrate the book — they distil its emotional core into a single frame."
We approach every cover through the lens of the genre market. A literary fiction cover that looks like a thriller is a broken promise. We study shelf context, bestseller visual language, and the specific reader who should feel seen by this cover — then we design against that brief with precision. Typography carries equal weight. Title lettering, subtitle hierarchy, author name scale — all calibrated to both digital thumbnail (where most books are first discovered) and print shelf. We deliver layered source files alongside final exports, so publishers retain full control at every future touchpoint.
INTERIOR SPREAD PLACEHOLDER

Fig. 2 — Chapter opener with drop cap ornament and full-bleed illustration.

Interior Illustration as Narrative Architecture

Where covers attract, interior illustrations deepen. We design illustration systems that work in dialogue with the text — never decorating, always meaning.

Each spot illustration is positioned to punctuate rhythm, not interrupt it. Chapter openers create anticipation. Full-bleed spreads create pause. The reader doesn't notice the design — they just feel the story working.

How We Work

A precise, collaborative four-stage process — structured so you're never waiting without reason.

01

Brief & Discovery

We read the manuscript (or synopsis), study your genre shelf, define the reader avatar, and agree on the visual direction before any artwork begins.

02

Concept & Storyboard

Mood boards, thumbnail sketches and storyboard panels are delivered for approval. This is the creative alignment stage — changes here cost nothing.

03

Illustration & Refinement

Final artwork is produced in stages — linework first, then colour, then typography. Two rounds of revisions are included at each stage.

04

Delivery & Handoff

Print-ready files (CMYK 300 DPI) and digital exports delivered in full. Source files included. We stay available for 30 days post-delivery for minor adjustments.
