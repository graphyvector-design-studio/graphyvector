Our Process

How we approach
every project.

The same discipline applied to a logo and a product launch film. Clear stages, honest communication, no surprises.

01

Brief & Discovery

We ask the questions most studios skip. Market position, audience, competitors, shelf context, file formats needed. Brief clarity prevents revision loops.

02

Concept & Direction

Two or three directions presented — not twelve. Each one is a committed point of view, not a hedge. You pick the direction that fits your brand truth.

03

Production & Refinement

The approved direction is built to production standard with two rounds of refinement included. We work in the actual delivery format from day one — no surprises at export.

04

Handoff & Beyond

Source files, usage guidelines and a 30-day post-delivery window for minor tweaks. We want the work to live and grow — not sit in a folder untouched.
