How we build it.

Four deliberate stages. Every step is documented and shared with you so there are no surprises, only decisions.

01

Discovery & Brand Audit

We start by auditing your existing visual presence — what you have, what's working and what's hurting you. Then we run a structured brief to understand your audience, competitors and the feeling you want your brand to own.

Brand Brief Competitor Audit Audience Mapping Visual Benchmarking
02

Visual Direction & Moodboarding

We present two distinct visual directions — not just color swatches, but full moodboards covering typography treatment, icon language, layout style and motion references. You choose the direction that resonates. We iterate until it's right.

Moodboards 2 Visual Territories Typography Pairing Color Psychology
03

Identity System Design

With direction locked, we design the full system — primary logo, variations, grid system, a complete icon set, layout language and usage rules. Everything is built on a geometry grid so it scales perfectly at any size.

Logo Construction Icon Set Grid System Motion-Ready Shapes
04

Handover & Brand Guidelines

Final delivery includes a brand guidelines PDF, an editable Figma component library, export-ready logo files in every format your team needs, and a social media launch kit so your first public post lands with the right weight.

Brand PDF Figma Library SVG / PNG / AI / EPS Social Launch Kit

Why it works

01

Scales from 16px to 16 feet

Every logo is built on a strict geometric grid. It looks identical whether it's a favicon, a business card or a trade show banner — because geometry doesn't lie.

02

Flexible, not random

The system has strict rules and a layout language that keeps everything consistent — but enough room for your team to produce new content without calling us every time.

03

Motion-ready from the start

Shapes and letterforms are chosen so they animate cleanly in Lottie or After Effects — no visual noise, no awkward in-betweens. Your brand can move if it needs to.
